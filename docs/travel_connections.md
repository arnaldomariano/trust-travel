# Travel Connections

Status: Draft / Work in progress

## 1. Product vision

Trust Travel transforms real travel experiences and verified information into collective knowledge that helps people plan better and travel with more confidence.

Travel Connections are part of this vision.

They do not replace the geographic hierarchy of places. Instead, they describe how places become relevant to each other from a traveler's perspective.

A geographic hierarchy answers:

> Where is this place located?

A Travel Connection answers:

> Why might a traveler visiting one place also care about another place?

Examples:

- Milan → Lake Como
- Florence → a winery in Tuscany
- São Luiz do Paraitinga → Cachoeira Grande do Fachinal
- Paraty → Trindade
- Rome → Vatican City
- Puerto Natales → Torres del Paine

The goal is not merely to link two places. The goal is to organize useful travel knowledge between them.

---

## 2. Core principles

### 2.1 Geographic hierarchy remains authoritative

Travel Connections must never change the geographic or administrative hierarchy of a Place.

Examples:

- A waterfall located in Lagoinha must remain under Lagoinha.
- A winery located outside Florence must remain under its correct geographic parent.
- Vatican City remains a separate country even when strongly connected to Rome.

The existing hierarchy continues to answer where a place officially belongs.

---

### 2.2 Travel Connections form a separate network

The geographic structure is a tree:

```text
Country
└── City / Region
    └── Specific Place
```
